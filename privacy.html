Overview

ZoomToOBS is a Windows desktop application that uses the Zoom Meeting SDK to join meetings and capture participant video locally for output to OBS Studio via Spout.

Data we collect

We do not operate servers and do not collect personal data from users.

The application runs entirely on your computer. You may enter or store locally:

  • Zoom Meeting SDK JWT (config.json)
  • Meeting ID, passcode, or invite link (app UI)
  • Display name shown in the meeting

These values remain on your device only.

How video data is handled

  • Video and screen share frames are processed locally in memory.
  • Output is sent to OBS via Spout on the same machine.
  • No meeting content is uploaded to developer servers or third parties by the app.

Zoom services

Use of Zoom is subject to the Zoom Privacy Policy. The app accesses meeting content only after the host grants recording permissions required by Zoom.

Third-party software

  • Zoom Meeting SDK
  • Spout2 (local GPU sharing)
  • OBS Studio (installed separately by the user)

Data retention

Configuration and logs stay on your device until you delete them. You may remove config.json at any time.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
